```
"Illusions"

Be honest
With Yourself.
Drop the Illusion You are holding
on to.
You have nothing to lose and
nothing to cling to.
You are exposed, out in the open air
of life,
laid bare
before a crowd or a desolate
patchwork of quilted tress, shrubs, desert plants, and minor things.
It is dark now; the midnight sky and moon call to you.
Look up, blink into your beholding gaze the crystal
stars and gulp in the crisp air.
This is where you belong.
```
